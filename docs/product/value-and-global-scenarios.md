# Farmer value and global scenario acceptance

The practical promise is **compare dated crop plans before committing resources, understand the water tradeoff, and know what to check next**. The product does not yet support a global claim of accurate field yield, optimal sowing date, net profit or calibrated loss probability. This document defines product acceptance and proposed QA cases, not new model parameters or observed validation results.

Capability audit baseline: `54a94d4`. The positioning notes supplied by the user describe the desired business decisions; their example money, percentages, hybrids and machinery delays are illustrations and must never seed production results. Keep five destinations (Fields, Strategies, Agent, Analytics, Farm), the contextual assistant, field selection and saved work intact.

## Prioritized decision insights

Each insight needs the selected field/boundary, applicable dates, evidence freshness, a plain-language conclusion and one useful next action. Show concise reasons first; reveal assumptions, charts and source identities below. The following are acceptance requirements and implementation opportunities, not a claim that every derived card already exists.

| Priority / farmer question | Inputs and calculation | Useful conclusion and next action | Limits / failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 — Which of my plans meets my goal and resources? | Actual decision values, priority used, plan feasibility, blockers/unknowns, interpretation envelope and rank. Show tied ranks as ties. | “First among these feasible plans for [criterion]”; explain one actual advantage and one actual cost/tradeoff. Open plan details or resolve its blocker. | Require `ranking_usable`, a usable selected criterion and actual feasible rank. No priority/missing land/thermal review/missing metric means comparison without recommendation. Fewer plans means fewer cards, never generated filler. |
| P0 — What must I check before sowing? | Calendar kind/precision, crop/cycle, selected planting date, actual weather issue and local hourly/daily conditions. | Distinguish “regional reference month” from “reviewed window”; surface an actual near-term frost/temperature/rain signal separately. Inspect current weather/soil or choose a dated comparison. | No automatic day choice from month bounds; no soil trafficability or germination guarantee from air weather. Past windows remain visible but cannot become a future action without a new date. |
| P0 — Where is water constraining this plan? | Actual source seasonal deficit/demand/use, daily reserve and its exact threshold, contiguous episode dates, irrigation schedule, soil depth and opening state. | Identify the modeled limiting cycle/episode, show its duration and source-defined threshold; inspect Water or compare an explicitly edited irrigation assumption. | Preserve source percentages, nulls and model terminology. Do not sum overlapping stress/waterlogging classes, infer physical deficit from a stress-day percentage, or call modeled reserve a measurement. |
| P0 — Why is a recommendation unavailable? | Specific missing/stale/unlinked land, soil, regional calendar, model support, economics or resource evidence. | One primary blocker plus direct action: inspect profile, refresh boundary inputs, select sourced calendar, confirm access, add assumptions or retry failed job. Calendar/allocation remain available where appropriate. | Never display zero risk or zero cost because a value is missing. No empty “all clear” state; no rank merely because a job succeeded. |
| P1 — What should I arrange before procurement? | Selected crop/calendar/cycle, actual required equipment and access states, irrigation availability, assigned area and existing commitments. | A plan-specific preparation list: confirm an unknown machine/access, arrange unavailable equipment or compare a feasible alternative; review crop areas before an order. | Access is not machine capacity, delivery availability or a schedule. No seed/fertilizer amount without a supplied rate and compatible unit; no invented supplier, price, quote or order action. |
| P1 — What could this plan earn under my assumptions? | Per-cycle marketable yield input, moisture basis, farm-gate price, included costs, currency and source. Revenue/ha = yield t/ha × price/currency per t. Margin/ha = revenue/ha − included costs/ha. | Show supplied/reference assumptions and missing cost categories; compare equal-area/equal-period scenarios or ask for the missing values. | Not a predicted yield, net profit or ROI. Mixed currencies and incomplete cycles cannot yield a comparable monetary total. Current handoff draft does not expose all schema economic inputs; an input affordance is required before treating this as a complete user flow. |
| P1 — How fragile is the financial choice? | User-defined low/base/high price, yield and cost assumptions applied to the same dated plans and units. | Show scenario ranges and whether the preferred plan changes; break-even price = included costs / positive yield, or yield = included costs / positive price. | Proposed deterministic sensitivity feature, not an existing calibrated interval. Zero denominator stays undefined. No arbitrary ±20% defaults or probabilities. Missing fixed/logistics/finance costs remain explicit. |
| P1 — When should I revisit the plan? | Forecast expiry/new issue, changed boundary/profile, changed calendar/price/equipment assumption, saved job identity and commitments. | “Refresh weather before the operation” or “Recalculate after this input change”; preserve draft and explain what changed. | A new field or boundary cannot reuse a result as current. Human-confirmed apply remains separate; failed request retry must not duplicate commitments. |

Pros and cons must follow actual comparable values: “less scheduled irrigation” can accompany “lower supplied revenue”, when both exist. A lower water deficit alone does not justify “more profitable”; more cover days does not establish a sustainable farming score. Handoff `water_saving` minimizes **scheduled irrigation mm**, `water_stress` uses its actual day-share metric, `water_deficit` its returned percentage, and `soil_cover` living-cover days. Display the exact objective rather than renaming all of them “low risk”.

An optional conversion of water depth to volume is `m³ = mm × hectares × 10`, only for the same field area, period and depth quantity. A modeled crop deficit is not automatically a pumping requirement: application efficiency, timing, access and irrigation limits are separate inputs. Multiplying a seasonal total across overlapping saved jobs double-counts water. A field's crop-area × seeding-rate procurement quantity requires a genuine user/source rate; the current model catalog does not establish one.

For nearby planting decisions, the role of local soil condition and forecast trend is supported by [Iowa State's 2026 guidance](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/post/soil-temperature-and-planting-conditions-what-watch-spring). It is regional guidance, not a universal temperature rule. [FAO-56](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm) separates weather, crop and management effects in crop-water accounting; its reference parameters do not validate this app's field accuracy. Even [ECMWF's sub-seasonal products](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/about-our-forecasts/sub-seasonal-range-forecasts) describe weekly conditions/anomalies, not exact daily farm outcomes years ahead. These sources support the presentation boundaries, not new imported measurements or claimed app integrations.

Run common overlays across representative cases, rather than creating 24 separate business algorithms:

- **Land provenance:** no links, unrelated weather link absent, partial coverage, point sample, stale/future source, changed boundary, synthetic source, and valid explicitly linked full-polygon evidence. Only the qualified case may satisfy the land gate; that still does not prove crop suitability.
- **Dates and weather:** local midnight, DST transition, leap day, end-of-year, no full day, provider lag, missing hour/day, dry zero versus missing, current issue versus historical selection; preserve exact applicable period. Historical median requires the promised complete years, not a padded series.
- **Water:** source zero and null, strict threshold equality, two separated stress episodes, overlapping stress categories, irrigation access unknown/false/true. A percentage remains the source percentage; it cannot be reconstructed from an incompatible total.
- **Economics and resources:** missing price/cost, zero yield or price, incompatible currencies, excluded equipment, unknown access, differing cycle counts, locked crop and insufficient area. Do not rank incomparable totals as if equal annual outcomes; do not silently relax constraints.
- **Durability and scope:** pending job, terminal failure, lost response/exact retry, explicit rerun, page reload, field change during polling, boundary edit during execution, unrelated farm/job. Never expose another context's result or silently duplicate a plan/application.
- **Presentation:** Ukrainian/English; keyboard; touch widths 320/390/768 and desktop; long translated crop names; null and diagnostic-rich results. No horizontal page overflow, hidden main action or modal that prevents access to evidence/assistant. Calendar and advanced allocation remain reachable from missing-model state.
